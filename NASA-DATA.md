# NASA data and resources

Everything from NASA that this project uses, how it is used and how it is
credited. This text feeds the "NASA Data" field of the Space Apps submission
form, the README and the in-game credits screen.

## NASA Exoplanet Archive

- **What:** the Planetary Systems Composite Parameters table (`pscomppars`),
  one row per confirmed exoplanet.
- **Citation:** NASA Exoplanet Archive, Planetary Systems Composite Parameters
  table. DOI: 10.26133/NEA13. Accessed 2026-11-14.
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/
- **Acknowledgment:** the archive asks every published work that uses it to
  include its standard acknowledgment sentence. Copy it verbatim from
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/acknowledge.html into the
  README and the credits screen.
- **How we use it:** every planet in the game is a planet from this table. Its
  real radius, mass, equilibrium temperature, host star and distance drive how
  the planet looks and plays.
- **Local copy:** `web/public/data/exoplanets.csv`, written by
  `web/scripts/download-exoplanets.mjs` (run `npm run data` inside `web/`).
  The file next to it, `exoplanets.meta.json`, records the query, the access
  date and the row count.
- **Columns kept:** `pl_name`, `hostname`, `sy_pnum`, `discoverymethod`,
  `disc_year`, `pl_rade`, `pl_bmasse`, `pl_dens`, `pl_eqt`, `pl_insol`,
  `pl_orbper`, `pl_orbsmax`, `pl_orbeccen`, `st_teff`, `st_rad`, `st_mass`,
  `st_spectype`, `sy_dist`, `ra`, `dec`.
- **What is derived, not real:** colours, terrain, weather and creatures are
  generated by the game from those numbers. The archive holds no surface data
  or images. Describe them as "derived from real data".

## NASA 3D Resources

- **What:** NASA's public collection of 3D models,
  https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
- **Usage guidelines:** https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
- **How we use it:** real spacecraft, rovers, antennas and tools are the
  reference for models rebuilt in the game's own style. The original files are
  not shipped in the game.
- **Credit line per model:** `[Model name], NASA 3D Resources. Credit: NASA.
  Adapted by the team; NASA insignia removed.` If the model's own page names a
  centre (for example NASA/JPL-Caltech), use that credit instead.

## A Learning Game About Telescopes

- **What:** NASA's educational game "Build It Yourself: Satellite!", from the
  James Webb Space Telescope site, and one of the official resources of the
  challenge. https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/game-build-it-yourself/
- **How we use it:** as the design reference for the suit visor. What the
  player wants to find out about a planet decides which instruments they
  need, under limits. We take the idea and the structure, not its text, art
  or code.
- **Credit:** NASA.

## Canadian Space Agency (Space Agency Partner)

Not NASA data: these go in the "Space Agency Partner Data and Other
References" field of the form.

- **What:** the CSA's Digital Games and Activities page, one of the official
  resources of the challenge.
  https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/youth-educators/digital-games-and-activities.asp
- **How we use it:** the OSIRIS-REx game as a design reference for the suit
  visor, and the 3D printing models as shape references for models rebuilt in
  the game's style. Nothing is used yet; add a line here for each item once it
  is.
- **Terms:** non-commercial use is allowed when the CSA is acknowledged as the
  source and nothing implies its endorsement. The CSA logo and the symbols of
  the Government of Canada may not be used.
- **Credit line:** `[Item name]. Credit: Canadian Space Agency.` If the item
  names another owner (for example "Credit: NASA"), use that credit.

## Other references

Not NASA data: these also go in the "Space Agency Partner Data and Other
References" field.

- **Three.js** (MIT licence), https://threejs.org: the 3D engine. The kit
  also uses its add-ons RoundedBoxGeometry, BufferGeometryUtils,
  OrbitControls and GLTFExporter.
- **Vite** (MIT licence) and **TypeScript** (Apache 2.0 licence): build tools.
- **Star colours** (`web/src/data/derived.ts`): Planck's law, the CIE 1931
  colour matching functions in the analytic fit of C. Wyman, P.-P. Sloan and
  P. Shirley, "Simple Analytic Approximations to the CIE XYZ Color Matching
  Functions", *Journal of Computer Graphics Techniques* 2(2), 2013,
  https://jcgt.org/published/0002/02/01/, and the sRGB standard
  (IEC 61966-2-1) to turn them into screen colours.

## Rules we follow

- NASA is acknowledged as the source and nothing implies NASA endorses the
  game.
- No NASA insignia, logotype, flags or mission identifiers appear in the game,
  in generated images or in the demo.
- Anything generated with AI tools is disclosed as the submission guide
  requires.
