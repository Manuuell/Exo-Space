# Name of the game

## Chosen, for now

### **Exo Space: A Way Home**

Decided by the team on 2026-11-14 as a provisional name. "Exo Space" says the
game is about exoplanets; "A Way Home" is the story: finding the way home.

**Worth knowing:** Steam has *eXoSpace Combat Engineer*, a spaceship game
released in September 2026. For a non-commercial hackathon it breaks no rule
and the subtitle tells them apart. If the game were ever published on a store,
it would need a new name.

## Other ideas that came up

None convinced the team; they stay here in case they help as a starting point.

|---|---|---|

| Exo Mariner | The Mariner probes, the first to visit other planets | Free on Steam and on the web |
| Exo Ulysses | Ulysses' journey back home | Free on Steam and on the web |
| Free Return | The trajectory that brings a spacecraft back to Earth without engines | Free on Steam |
| Third From Sol | Earth, the third planet from the Sun | Free on Steam |

## Discarded because they are taken

Exoworlds (an MMORPG on the Epic Games Store), Parsec, Perihelion, Oort,
Oumuamua, Halley, Pale Dots and Exo Frontier.

## Before fixing the final name

- Search for it on Steam, itch.io and the web in general.
- Avoid names of NASA missions (Kepler, TESS, Artemis): the hackathon guide
  forbids mission identifiers in generated content.
